Scope and non-goals
--------------------

- This is an EVAL-ONLY diagnostic. There is no promotion, no training, no
  self-play, and no gradient step of any kind performed under this
  preregistration. `promotion=false` and `training=false` and
  `self_play=false` are structural properties of the seed package, not
  optional flags a caller can flip.
- There is no hard pass/fail gate. Outcomes are classification-only: for each
  pre-specified comparison pair we report the sign/magnitude of the paired
  difference together with its pre-specified confidence interval, and we
  classify the result (e.g. "BC retains/improves/regresses relative to
  teacher", "aux changes/does not change relative to BC") without declaring
  a binary PASS/FAIL verdict. Any future decision to act on these numbers
  (e.g. start a new training round) requires its own, separately
  preregistered document.
- Comparisons are restricted to four fixed, pre-selected checkpoints (see
  below) plus one fixed learned Spawner. No other checkpoint may be
  substituted in, and no checkpoint may be swapped out after seeing results.

Common-random-number (CRN) pairing for learned-Spawner stochasticity
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The learned Spawner is a stochastic policy (`Normal`/`Categorical` heads).
To keep player-vs-player comparisons statistically paired rather than
independently noisy, every player arm (`v1_teacher`, `v5_bc_seed6`,
`v5_aux_u200`) is evaluated against the *same* fixed learned Spawner using
the *same* per-seed common random numbers:

- the same environment seed list (CRN environment seeds) is reused,
  unchanged, across all three player arms;
- when the Spawner's action mode is stochastic (`sample_policy=True`), the
  same per-episode Spawner sampling seed is reused across arms for that
  seed slot (via `torch.random.fork_rng()` keyed on the episode seed, as
  already implemented in `qrokkun_env.eval_modes.run_eval_episode`), so
  Spawner action draws line up seed-for-seed across arms instead of drifting
  independently;
- CRN pairing only compares *within* a seed slot (arm A seed i vs arm B seed
  i); it never mixes seed slots.

This is what makes the paired differences (below) meaningful: any variance
from a stochastic learned Spawner is a shared nuisance factor cancelled by
pairing, not conflated with the player-arm effect being measured.

Locked action modes, seeds, cap, and loader contract
--------------------------------------------------------

- Action modes are drawn only from the three named modes already defined in
  `qrokkun_env.eval_modes` (`MODE_DET_DET`, `MODE_DET_STOCH`,
  `MODE_STOCH_STOCH`); `(sample_policy=True, rng_jitter=False)` remains
  categorically invalid and is rejected the same way it already is by
  `validate_eval_mode`. The player action mode and the Spawner action mode
  are each one of these named modes and are recorded explicitly in the seed
  package; they are locked before launch and never changed mid-run.
- CRN environment seeds are drawn from the existing paired-eval seed table
  (`qrokkun_env.eval_modes.PAIRED_EVAL_SEEDS`, i.e. `range(3000, 3030)`,
  30 seeds) unless a future preregistration explicitly documents a
  different, equally-fixed seed list (see "alternate seed changes the
  calibration window" below).
- Episode cap is locked at the same contract already used across PlayerV5
  eval rounds: max 4,200 frames per episode (70 s wall-clock at the existing
  env `dt`), reported as `min(elapsed, cap)` per seed together with a
  censor flag (censor = episode reached the cap without the player being
  hit; non-censor = the player was hit before the cap).
- The checkpoint loader contract for `v1_teacher` (legacy `PlayerV1`, no
  architecture metadata) and for `v5_bc_seed6`/`v5_aux_u200`
  (`PlayerRankedTopK`, loaded strictly via
  `qrokkun_env.agents.player_checkpoints.load_ranked_top_k_checkpoint`, which
  enforces schema version, action list, observation dims, and
  `state_dict_sha256`) is unchanged by this document. The learned Spawner is
  loaded via the existing `SpawnerV4` checkpoint dict contract (`d_model`,
  `hidden`, `state_dict`) used by `corner_probe.load_spawner` /
  `render_v4_demo.py`; this preregistration does not introduce a new
  Spawner-checkpoint schema.

Metrics
-------

Primary (computed independently for each of the three player arms, and as
paired differences for the two pre-specified comparison pairs):

- restricted mean survival: `mean(min(elapsed_i, cap))` over all seeds;
- median of `min(elapsed_i, cap)`;
- std and min/max of `min(elapsed_i, cap)`;
- censor count (episodes that reached the cap without being hit);
- per-seed values (`min(elapsed_i, cap)`, hit/censor flag) for full
  transparency and reproducibility of any downstream re-aggregation.

Pre-specified comparison pairs (paired on CRN seed slot, computed against
the identical fixed learned Spawner):

1. `v1_teacher` vs `v5_bc_seed6` ("V1-vs-BC");
2. `v5_bc_seed6` vs `v5_aux_u200` ("BC-vs-aux").

For each pair: per-seed difference, paired mean/median difference, and a
pre-specified confidence interval (paired 95% t-interval on the per-seed
differences, matching the interval already used in the retention-aux
report). No other pairing (e.g. `v1_teacher` vs `v5_aux_u200` directly) is a
primary metric under this document; it may be reported as a purely
descriptive secondary note but carries no pre-specified CI.

Secondary (descriptive only, no CI required, no gate):

- hit rate (fraction of seeds where the player was hit before the cap);
- termination-reason breakdown to the extent the env exposes it;
- early-death rate (fraction of seeds with `elapsed` below a short
  descriptive threshold, e.g. < 5 s), reported purely as a diagnostic of
  qualitatively bad episodes, not as a gate input.

No hard pass/fail gate
------------------------

There is no retention floor, no minimum-ratio threshold, and no
accept/reject rule attached to any of the metrics above. The output of this
eval is a classification of each pre-specified comparison ("higher",
"lower", "indistinguishable given the CI", together with the numbers), not a
verdict. Any subsequent action (further training, promotion, or rollback)
requires a new, separate preregistration that consumes these numbers as
input, exactly as prior PlayerV5 rounds have required for each new decision.

Later BC-start and PPO-trajectory replication boundaries
-------------------------------------------------------------

This preregistration only covers the frozen-Spawner, eval-only comparison
described above. It explicitly does **not** cover, and must not be read as
authorizing, any of the following without a further, separate
preregistration:

- a later BC-start replication that re-runs behavior cloning from a
  different teacher dataset draw or a different seed-6-equivalent starting
  state — any such replication is a new experiment with its own dataset
  identity hash, split contract, and initial-gate check;
- a later replication that preserves the corrected BC-retention auxiliary
  PPO contract in full (the auxiliary term is not dropped) and changes only
  the pre-registered PPO trajectory inputs — e.g. an alternate rollout seed
  window or an alternate action-sampling seed — against the learned Spawner
  (or against scripted Spawner); this is out of scope here regardless of
  which trajectory inputs change, because this document authorizes no PPO
  updates at all, of any stream;
- boundary condition on rollout/action seeds: if a later round uses an
  alternate rollout seed window or an alternate action-sampling seed
  (i.e. anything other than the exact seed schedule locked in the round it
  replicates), that changes the update-1 calibration window, because the
  existing contract reuses update 1's on-policy rollout, unmodified, as the
  one-shot alpha calibration sample (`CALIBRATION_GRAD_SAMPLES=8`,
  frozen once, never recollected). A different seed schedule is a different
  calibration draw and must be treated as a new calibration, not assumed
  equivalent to a prior round's frozen alpha;
- the target retention gradient ratio remains `TARGET_RETENTION_GRAD_RATIO
  = 0.15` unchanged across any such replication; it is a design ratio, not a
  quantity that is re-derived from data;
- alpha itself, however, is *not* frozen across replications: because alpha
  is `0.15 * mean(||g_ppo||) / (mean(||g_ret||) + 1e-8)` computed from the
  specific calibration rollout of the specific run, a replication with a
  different starting checkpoint, different teacher dataset, or different
  calibration seed window may — and in general will — recalibrate alpha to
  a different numeric value under the same target ratio. Reusing a
  previously-frozen alpha across an incompatible calibration window would be
  a silent contract violation.

Seed package / manifest contract (this repo's new code)
-------------------------------------------------------------

To make the above machine-checkable rather than only documented in prose,
this round introduces a small, eval-only, immutable "seed package" /
manifest object and validator (`qrokkun_env/frozen_spawner_seed_package.py`,
unit-tested in
`qrokkun_env/tests/test_frozen_spawner_seed_package.py`). It:

- makes every input above explicit and machine-checked: the four fixed
  checkpoint roles (path + SHA-256 placeholders/records), architecture per
  role, player and Spawner action modes (one of the three named
  `qrokkun_env.eval_modes` modes each), CRN environment seeds, CRN Spawner
  sampling seeds, episode cap, the restricted metric set, and the two
  pre-specified comparison pairs with their CI method;
- is an immutable (frozen) value object once constructed — attempted
  mutation raises, matching the "no post-result selection" requirement;
- rejects malformed packages (bad SHA-256 shape, unknown checkpoint role,
  action mode not one of the three named modes, non-positive/mismatched
  seed lists, comparison pair referencing an undefined role, metric name
  outside the restricted allow-list) and rejects conflicting packages
  (e.g. Spawner action mode stochastic but no Spawner sampling seeds
  supplied);
- structurally forbids promotion/training/self-play: the constructor does
  not expose a way to set `promotion`, `training`, or `self_play` to
  anything other than `False`, so there is no flag a caller could flip to
  turn this into a training or promotion artifact;
- produces a JSON-serializable manifest dict (`to_json_dict()`), suitable
  for writing alongside any future eval report, with only plain
  `str`/`int`/`float`/`bool`/`list`/`dict` values.

This module has no dependency on any real checkpoint file existing on disk;
its unit tests use synthetic SHA-256-shaped placeholder strings and tiny/no
neural nets, exactly like the existing `qrokkun_env.eval_modes` tests. Any
eval-runner primitives added alongside it (deterministic restricted-survival
statistics, paired-difference statistics) are pure functions over
already-computed elapsed-time lists and are exercised only with synthetic
data in unit tests — no actual eval against `v1_teacher` / `v5_bc_seed6` /
`v5_aux_u200` / a real learned Spawner is run or required by this
preregistration or by its tests.

Real eval wiring (this round's new code)
-------------------------------------------

The seed package and stats primitives above deliberately stopped short of
actually loading a checkpoint or running an episode. This round adds that
missing wiring as a new, separate module,
`qrokkun_env/frozen_spawner_eval_cli.py`, unit-tested in
`qrokkun_env/tests/test_frozen_spawner_eval_cli.py` (TDD: tests written and
observed RED against the not-yet-existing module, then made to pass). It:

- loads each checkpoint role via a fail-closed, architecture-dispatched
  loader (`load_checkpoint_for_role`): the on-disk file SHA-256 is
  recomputed and compared against the pinned value *before* `torch.load` is
  ever called, and, when a `state_dict_sha256` is recorded, it is
  cross-checked against the loader's own freshly computed state-dict hash
  before the net is handed back to any caller — any mismatch raises
  `ChecksumMismatchError` and loads nothing;
- uses the correct, distinct loader/observation contract per role: legacy
  `PlayerV1` (dict checkpoint, flat 45-dim `qrokkun_env.obs.vectorize` obs)
  is never confused with `PlayerRankedTopK` (strict metadata checkpoint via
  `qrokkun_env.agents.player_checkpoints`, masked player/bullets/pad obs via
  `qrokkun_env.agents.obs_v4.encode_obs`); the frozen Spawner uses the
  existing `SpawnerV4` checkpoint-dict contract (`d_model`/`hidden`/
  `state_dict`), introducing no new Spawner-checkpoint schema;
- runs one eval-only episode per (player arm, seed) via
  `run_frozen_spawner_episode`, with *independent* player/Spawner sampling
  flags — the player is always evaluated deterministically (argmax) while
  the Spawner may be deterministic or stochastic per the manifest's
  `spawner_action_mode`. This is deliberately not implemented by reusing
  `qrokkun_env.train.both_v4.run_episode` directly, because that function
  ties both player and Spawner sampling to a single shared `sample` flag,
  which cannot represent "deterministic player, stochastic Spawner" at the
  same time;
- reproduces CRN pairing via `run_frozen_spawner_episode_crn`, which forks
  the Torch RNG keyed on the per-slot seed only when sampling actually
  consumes it, mirroring `qrokkun_env.eval_modes.run_eval_episode`'s
  reproducibility contract — re-running the same player arm at the same
  seed slot against the same stochastic Spawner always reproduces the same
  elapsed time, independent of any other arm's run or of unrelated global
  RNG mutation in between;
- runs exactly the two preregistered comparison pairs (`v1_teacher` vs
  `v5_bc_seed6`, `v5_bc_seed6` vs `v5_aux_u200`) via
  `run_frozen_spawner_comparison`, producing a JSON-serializable report with
  per-seed elapsed times, restricted survival statistics per arm, and the
  paired-difference statistics for each preregistered pair — no other
  pairing is computed;
- is structurally eval-only: the module imports no optimizer
  (`torch.optim`), calls no PPO update function, launches no self-play, and
  selects/writes no "promoted" checkpoint; the report's `promotion`/
  `training`/`self_play` fields are hardcoded `False` literals, and
  `run_frozen_spawner_comparison` refuses (raises) if the manifest's player
  action mode is not deterministic;
- exposes a small CLI (`main`) that takes checkpoint paths + pinned
  file-SHA-256 values, plus an *optional* pinned state-dict-SHA-256 per
  role, builds the seed package, runs the comparison, and writes the
  manifest + report JSON files to disk. When a state-dict SHA-256 pin is
  supplied for a role, `load_checkpoint_for_role` fails closed (raises
  `ChecksumMismatchError`, loading nothing usable) if the loaded net's own
  recomputed state-dict hash does not match it — this is what catches a
  Spawner/PlayerV5 state-dict swap or corruption that a file-level hash
  alone would not, since the file hash only covers the on-disk checkpoint
  dict wrapper, not the exact tensor values a caller intended to pin;
- the seed package itself (not just the CLI) now structurally enforces the
  fixed role→architecture mapping (`v1_teacher` -> `player_v1`,
  `v5_bc_seed6`/`v5_aux_u200` -> `player_ranked_topk`, `learned_spawner` ->
  `spawner_v4`) and the exact two preregistered comparison pairs in their
  registered order (`v1_teacher` vs `v5_bc_seed6`, then `v5_bc_seed6` vs
  `v5_aux_u200`) — both are rejected by
  `FrozenSpawnerManifestError` at construction time, before any checkpoint
  load, so a manifest naming the wrong architecture for a role, or an
  altered/extra/reordered comparison-pair list, can never reach the loader
  or the runner.

All unit tests for this module use tiny synthetic checkpoints packed to
`tmp_path` inside the test module itself; none of them touch a real
NAS/production checkpoint path, and none of them are pointed at the
`learned_spawner` candidate identity recorded above. No eval has been
launched under this preregistration using real artifacts — only the CLI/
module itself has been implemented and unit-tested with synthetic data.

Formal-contract corrections (this round, review-blocker fixes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

A strict-review pass identified six functional gaps between this document's
prose and the actual eval-only code (`qrokkun_env/frozen_spawner_eval_cli.py`,
`qrokkun_env/frozen_spawner_eval_runner.py`,
`qrokkun_env/frozen_spawner_seed_package.py`); those gaps were closed by
tests-first (RED observed, then made GREEN) in
`qrokkun_env/tests/test_frozen_spawner_eval_review_blockers.py`. No episode
has been run against a real artifact and no PPO/training/self-play/
promotion path was touched by any of these corrections — they are all
either pure argparse/manifest-identity locks or pure statistics/data-shape
changes over the existing synthetic-checkpoint unit tests.

1. **Hard-locked formal CLI identity.** `frozen_spawner_eval_cli.build_parser()`
   now locks `--player-action-mode` to `det_det` and `--spawner-action-mode`
   to `stoch_stoch` only (argparse `choices=(<single value>,)`), and locks
   `--env-seed-start`/`--env-seed-count`/`--episode-cap-frames` to `3000`/
   `30`/`4200` only — i.e. exactly the CRN seed window
   `qrokkun_env.eval_modes.PAIRED_EVAL_SEEDS` (`3000..3029`) and the
   preregistered 4,200-frame cap. Any other value on any of these five
   flags is rejected by argparse (`SystemExit`) before a seed package is
   ever built; there is no silent coercion/clamping path. This closes the
   gap where the formal CLI could previously be invoked with an arbitrary
   mode/seed-window/cap combination and still emit a report that looked
   like it belonged to this preregistration. Internal/test-only helpers
   (`run_frozen_spawner_episode`, `build_frozen_spawner_seed_package`)
   remain independently configurable for fast synthetic unit tests, because
   they cannot themselves emit this CLI's formal manifest/report identity.
2. **Stable manifest/report identity.** `FrozenSpawnerSeedPackage` now
   carries two additional immutable fields, `contract_id` (locked to
   `"player_v5_frozen_spawner_eval_v1"`) and `preregistration_path` (locked
   to this file's repo-relative path,
   `docs/journal/1789263159_player_v5_frozen_spawner_eval_preregistration.txt`),
   both included in `to_json_dict()`. Direct construction with a different
   value for either field raises `FrozenSpawnerManifestError`, so every
   manifest/report produced by this module carries the same durable
   preregistration-document identifier regardless of which checkpoint
   paths/hashes/seeds a particular package instance recorded — exactly like
   the existing `schema_version` field, `contract_id`/`preregistration_path`
   identify *which preregistered document/schema* a manifest belongs to,
   not that its seeds/cap/modes match the CLI's stronger, independently-
   enforced canonical-parameter lock (correction 1 above). That stronger
   per-launch guarantee is enforced only by
   `frozen_spawner_eval_cli.build_parser()`'s argparse `choices`; the
   generic, intentionally-reusable seed-package module still allows
   smaller/faster synthetic seed lists and caps for its own and the CLI's
   unit tests.
3. **Player physics reuse, not a diverging copy.**
   `frozen_spawner_eval_cli.apply_player_action` now delegates directly to
   `qrokkun_env.train.both_v4.apply_player_action` (the single canonical,
   float32-rounded implementation) instead of keeping an independently
   maintained duplicate. The duplicate had drifted: it applied player
   position/velocity updates in plain Python floats, without the float32
   rounding (`qrokkun_env.godot_rng.f32`) both_v4 applies at every step,
   which would have silently diverged from the training-time physics over a
   long episode. `test_eval_apply_player_action_matches_both_v4_float32_
   physics_long_sequence` exercises 576 frames of mixed actions and asserts
   bit-for-bit agreement of `(px, py, pvx, pvy)` between the eval fork and
   `both_v4.apply_player_action` at every frame. Reusing this one pure,
   gradient-free physics function does not import or invoke any optimizer/
   PPO/self-play/promotion code from `both_v4`.
4. **Explicit structured episode outcome.** `run_frozen_spawner_episode`
   (and its CRN wrapper) now return a mapping with `elapsed`, `frames`,
