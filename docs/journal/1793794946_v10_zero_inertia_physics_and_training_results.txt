1. Root-Cause Diagnosis of the Legacy v5 (~36–43s) Ceiling
----------------------------------------------------------
During initial RL exploration on GB10 (`ovm-hms-bot@192.168.0.41`), we isolated three compounding bottlenecks that held `PlayerV5` around ~36–43s under the old codebase:
  (a) Shared Actor-Critic Backbone Interference: Under long horizons (`gamma=0.9995, lam=0.999`), shared-trunk value gradients washed out the policy representation (`EV <= 0`). Decoupling the Critic into an independent MLP (`reward_scale=0.05`) restored `EV` to `0.73–0.88`.
  (b) Single-Frame Myopia & Static Teacher Anchoring: Anchoring to the static `PlayerV1` teacher (`35.5s`) prevented the policy from surpassing the teacher, while raw `(dx, dy, vx, vy)` coordinates required the 2-layer MLP to implicitly integrate 8-way multi-step collision geometry. Adding explicit discrete-step kinematic clearance features (`batch_kinematic_clearances`) immediately jumped single-forward-pass MLP survival to ~60s.
  (c) Fatal Physics Defect — Velocity Turnaround Inertia (`PLAYER_ACCEL = 980.0`):
      Inspection of `scripts/player.gd` and `qrokkun_env/env.py` revealed an unintended asymmetry:
      - Releasing keys (`idle`) snapped velocity to `(0, 0)` in 1 frame.
      - Pressing or reversing direction used `move_toward(..., ACCEL * delta)` with `ACCEL = 980.0` (`16.33 px/s` per frame vs `MAX_SPEED = 102.0 px/s`).
      - Consequently, reversing direction (e.g., `e -> w`) took 12 frames (`0.20s`), during which the ship slid ~6 frames in the OLD direction before crossing zero speed.
      Owner confirmed that player movement was intended to be strictly zero-inertia (instantaneous single speed `102.0 px/s` on key press or direction change, instantaneous stop on release, normalized diagonal speed equal to cardinal speed, and constant bullet velocity after spawn).
