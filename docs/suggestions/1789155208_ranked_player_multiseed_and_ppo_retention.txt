Ranked Player architecture, multi-seed reproduction, and BC-retention PPO control
================================================================================

Status
------

The formal ranked top-k Player architecture was implemented and its supervised
reproducibility gate passed. The subsequent frozen/no-update versus current
scripted-only PPO retention control failed the pre-registered retention gate.
No PPO checkpoint is promoted.

The main finding is unambiguous: current scripted PPO rapidly moves the policy
away from the transferred PlayerV1 skill. Closed-loop survival is statistically
unchanged through update 100 on paired 30-seed comparisons, while held teacher
agreement collapses from 0.700 to 0.240. At update 200, agreement reaches 0.173
and survival falls to 24.53 s mean / 22.62 s median.

Scope and isolation
-------------------

- Player only, against the scripted Spawner.
- No learned Spawner, self-play, random-start fraction, entropy A/B,
  gamma/lambda A/B, reward rewrite, or DAgger.
- PlayerV1 trajectories were used only for supervised distillation and held
  diagnostics. They were never inserted into PPO on-policy batches.
- All NAS artifacts had already been copied locally before evaluation.

Formal Player architecture
--------------------------

PlayerRankedTopK preserves distinct ranked bullet slots rather than pooling all
bullets into one single-query attention context.

- architecture key: player_ranked_topk
- observation contract: player_feat=6, bullet_feat=9, max_bullets=64
- ranked slots retained: top_k=8
- action order: idle, n, ne, e, se, s, sw, w, nw
- hidden width: 472
- parameter count: 269,050
- parameter-count target: 269,050; relative error 0
- strict checkpoint schema validates architecture, observation metadata,
  action order, top_k, hidden width, state-dict shapes, and state-dict hash.
- supervised checkpoints are production-compatible but are not promoted until
  the reproducibility gate passes.
- PPO snapshots are explicitly experimental and non-production; they include
  parent checkpoint hashes, dataset hash, update, knobs, seed windows, and eval
  summary. They are rejected as production initialization checkpoints.

Retention verdict
-----------------

- mean retention: FAIL
- median retention: FAIL
- held teacher agreement retention: FAIL
- first breaking snapshot: update 10
- overall retention gate: FAIL
- promotion: false

Current scripted PPO does not retain the transferred BC skill. It temporarily
keeps survival statistically indistinguishable from the starting policy through
update 100 while rapidly destroying teacher agreement. By update 200, the
paired survival difference is also negative. This matched same-start comparison
associates the changes with the PPO arm; the frozen implementation itself only
proves that one evaluation does not mutate weights.

Limitations
-----------

- The retention control uses one representative BC training seed. It is a
  matched same-checkpoint causal control, not a claim about PPO variance across
  all three BC training seeds.
- The PPO arm itself is a single run (torch seed 0 and one rollout-seed stream
  beginning at 50000). The curve shape is not separable from PPO run-to-run
  variance. The agreement collapse from 0.700 to 0.173 is the large primary
  signal; intermediate survival point estimates are treated as noise-limited.
- Evaluation uses one fixed 30-seed scripted-Spawner contract. It does not test
  learned Spawners or live self-play.
- Agreement is measured on teacher-state held data. It detects retention loss,
  but not all student-state covariate shift.
- The 25/50/100 update survival peaks are diagnostic snapshots, not checkpoints
  selected by a preregistered promotion rule. They must not be promoted after
  observing their closed-loop outcomes.
- The harness mirrors historical PlayerV1 PPO truncation semantics. It does not
  test whether bootstrapping at truncation would change retention.

Decision and next step
----------------------

1. Promote the formal PlayerRankedTopK supervised architecture contract and
   record that its multi-seed reproduction gate passed.
2. Do not promote any checkpoint from the current scripted-only PPO arm.
3. Keep seed 6 BC as the representative retention baseline; retain all PPO
   snapshots only as experimental evidence.
4. The next controlled experiment should add a BC-retention auxiliary objective
   to scripted PPO, starting from the same seed 6 BC checkpoint and keeping the
   current PPO configuration, rollout seeds, frame/update budget, and eval
   contract fixed. Compare against this exact frozen and unregularized PPO
   result.
5. Pre-register retention weighting and selection using held imitation metrics;
   do not select update 25/50/100 retrospectively by survival.
6. Only after retention is controlled should entropy, gamma/lambda time scale,
   frozen/pool/lagged Spawner, or live self-play be introduced.
