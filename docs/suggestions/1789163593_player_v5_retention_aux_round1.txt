Interpretation
--------------

1. Round-1 α is too weak to hold the 0.6499 agreement floor. Update 10 already
   drops 0.6999 -> 0.6371 (drop 0.063 > 0.05). Gate FAIL as pre-registered.

2. Auxiliary loss is not a no-op versus unregularized PPO. Agreement plateaus
   near 0.615 instead of collapsing to 0.173. Update-200 survival stays at
   35.36 s mean / 38.03 s median instead of falling to 24.53 / 22.63.

3. Survival floors would have passed; they are not sufficient. Agreement is
   the first failure mode, same ordering as the unregularized control.

4. Calibration cosine was slightly negative, so the 15% gradient-ratio target
   is a lower bound on conflict, not an upper bound on retention strength.

5. Because agreement failed and closed-loop did not collapse, the next ticket
   is a pre-registered larger α only. Do not mix DAgger, entropy, gamma/lambda,
   or Spawner changes. Do not promote any snapshot from this run.
